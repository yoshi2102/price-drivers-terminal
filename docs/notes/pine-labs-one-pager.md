# Pine Labs (NSE: PINELABS): why we lean bullish

**Draft bull case, not yet a house view.** Our Digital Payments sector view ranks Pine Labs first on research merit [sector.md, 12-Nov-26], but there is no company.md, model, rating or target, and the coverage analyst has not signed off.

**Call:** constructive. At ₹161.79 [NSE close, 9-Nov-26] the market prices ~17.5% annual cash-flow growth after FY28. Paytm's price needs ~26% [sector.md reverse DCF, calc].

**What it does.** Pine Labs runs merchant checkout infrastructure (POS and soundbox terminals, EMI, online payments, fintech processing) and issues gift and corporate prepaid cards through Qwikcilver. It reports revenue **net** of interchange, so do not compare it with Paytm's gross revenue.

## Bull points
1. **Growth is speeding up and coming from several businesses.** Q2 FY27 revenue was ₹806 cr, up 24% YoY against 19.6% in Q1. Affordability/VAS GTV rose 52%, mid-market terminals 49% and issuing revenue 31% (India up 40%) [ICICI Sec, 4-Nov-26]. FY27 guide: above 23.5% [Emkay, 30-Oct-26].
2. **Clean earnings, with no credit risk.** With no lending book or default guarantees, the terminal business keeps 82.1% of revenue after direct costs [Emkay, 30-Oct-26]. FY26 operating cash flow, excluding early settlements, was ₹554 cr, 136% of core EBITDA. Net cash was ₹2,123 cr in Jun-26 [sector.md].
3. **Costs are growing slower than revenue.** EBITDA after ESOP was ₹139 cr, up 61% YoY on 24% revenue growth. ESOP fell from ₹29 cr to ₹23 cr [Emkay, 30-Oct-26].
4. **UPI MDR is extra, not in the base case.** Pine Labs processes ~₹20,000 cr of UPI a month, 65% of it above ₹2,000, and targets ₹33,000–34,000 cr by FY28. Brokers model ₹0–200 cr of FY28 MDR (Ambit and Investec model none) [ICICI Sec, 4-Nov-26; sector.md].

## Key risks
- **Contribution margin is falling.** It went from 78% to 72.8% between Q1 FY26 and Q2 FY27. About 675 new sales staff take roughly nine months to become productive [ICICI Sec, 4-Nov-26]. Bloomberg consensus FY27 PAT fell 7.6% in one month.
- **The MDR start date and pricing are open.** Start deferred to "after Diwali"; the CEO says merchants will not pay both device rent and MDR, and rent per device has already dipped to ₹330 a month [Emkay, 30-Oct-26].
- **Supply overhang.** PE funds hold 37.6% (Sep-26). H1 capex rose 2.8x to ₹240 cr on chip-shortage advances [sector.md; Emkay].

## What would make us wrong
- Contribution margin below 70% for two quarters.
- FY27 adjusted EBITDA margin below the 21% guide.
- No NPCI start date for MDR by 31-Mar-27.

The next check is Q3 FY27 results, due late Jan-27.
